## About

I build software that is usable on day one and composable on day one hundred.

My recent work sits at the intersection of:

- agent-native information systems
- AI-assisted consumer product workflows
- practical automation for real desktop and research work

The goal is straightforward: ship products with clear surfaces, credible internals, and enough operational discipline that other people can actually rely on them.

## Principles

- Build around concrete user workflows, not abstract demos.
- Prefer narrow products with strong taste over broad generic platforms.
- Keep public interfaces simple enough for humans and structured enough for agents.
- Write documentation that can sell the project and onboard the contributor.

## Current Focus

- shipping polished public-facing READMEs and project surfaces
- turning internal tools into durable open repositories
- improving the discoverability and usability of agent-native products
